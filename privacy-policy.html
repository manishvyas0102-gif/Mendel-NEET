Mendel NEET – Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Mendel NEET (package com.mendel.neetug) works entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

The name you enter, your goals, answers, saved questions and test results are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or using “Reset all progress” in Settings deletes them.

The app requests no device permissions and contains no advertising, analytics or third-party SDKs. It is suitable for students of all ages.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
